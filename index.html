DripLog Support

DripLog is a pour-over coffee timer and taste journal for iPhone.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas? Email saku0903.10@gmail.com. If something isn’t working, please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Questions

How do I get Premium back on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the Apple Account you used to buy it, open the Settings tab and tap Restore Purchase. You won’t be charged again.

What can I do with the free version?

Everything, with two limits: brews can be saved on up to 3 different days, and up to 3 kinds of beans can be added. After that you can still use the timer without saving. Premium is a one-time purchase that removes both limits. There is no subscription.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. It is included in your iPhone’s backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer). The app does not sync between devices, and deleting the app deletes its data.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
