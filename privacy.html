Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

DripLog does not collect, sell or share any personal information.

  • Your recipes, brews, beans and settings are stored only on your iPhone. The app has no servers, so we cannot see them.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking in the app.
  • If you use iCloud Backup, your device backup may include this data. Backups are handled by Apple.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.
  • When you share a brew, the image and text go only to the app you choose (for example Instagram, X or LINE) and are handled under that service’s privacy policy.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your iPhone.
